Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information before you choose your tank.

Are all Poly Tanks triple-layered?

Yes. All our tanks are triple-layered.

Do your tanks have UV protection?

Our tanks feature UV protection. Ask our team for specifications of your selected model.

Are the tanks food safe?

Our tanks are intended for water storage using food-safe materials. Request product specifications for confirmation.

Do you have different tank shapes?

Our range includes vertical, horizontal and rectangular tank designs. Availability varies by model and capacity.

Can I install a tank underground or on a rooftop?

Only use models approved for the intended installation. Underground burial and rooftop support must be properly assessed.
